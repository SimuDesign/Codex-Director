## Search lens

- Target: Codex Director core mark and native macOS app icon.
- Desired impression: premium, spatial, intelligent, calm, and finished enough
  to judge as a near-final visual rather than a wireframe.
- Locked color family: electric blue `#159DFF`, ice cyan `#49CAFF`, and mint
  `#79EAD8`, with deep navy/black application surfaces.
- GPT relationship: continuity, layered interdependence, and balanced negative
  space only; no OpenAI Blossom reproduction or endorsement implication.
- Rejected: monochrome-first gating, generic open loops, hub-and-spoke marks,
  literal CD monograms, flat geometric roughs, and diffusion outputs whose form
  is not anchored by an inspected reference.

## Approved process change

The decision owner explicitly removed the mandatory black-and-white silhouette
stage. After one lane below is approved, Brand Designer may generate the first
proposal directly at full visual fidelity with color, material, lighting, depth,

This changes proposal media, not identity approval: the generated result remains

## Reference A — Soft Chromatic Fold

Preview and source:

- [Microsoft 365 Copilot app — official Microsoft product page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/download-copilot-app)

Creator / platform: Microsoft Design / Microsoft.  
Inspected: current app icon in the hero, its pale-blue app environment, folded
multi-surface mark, highlight/shadow separation, and adjacent product imagery.

Visual observations:

- A compact folded ribbon is rendered as a soft spatial object rather than a
  flat outline.
- Carefully ordered overlaps, translucent edge lighting, and different color
  zones make a small symbol feel crafted without adding surface noise.
- Rounded geometry keeps the object approachable; restrained shadows establish
  depth while preserving an icon-like silhouette.
- The icon remains the focal object on a quiet, light-blue field.

Transferable grammar for Codex Director:

- two or three broad continuous surfaces with one clearly controlled over/under
  relationship;
- blue -> ice -> mint color travel, with highlights following the fold rather
  than a generic diagonal gradient;
- translucent rim light, soft inner shadow, and one crisp focal edge;
- a calm rounded app-icon field with generous negative space.

Do not copy:

- the Copilot loop/arch silhouette, exact fold sequence, Microsoft spectrum,
  M365 badge, or any recognizable Copilot proportion;
- four-color rainbow segmentation or a squared ribbon frame.

What choosing A means: the next proposal will be a soft, luminous, folded
spatial symbol whose refinement comes from material and overlap.  
Trade-off: this is the closest AI-category reading and therefore carries the
highest collision risk; originality must come from a different silhouette and
product-specific center structure.

## Reference B — Faceted Orchestration Crystal

Preview and source:

- [Kaya brand identity — Ruckus case study](https://www.ruckusmarketing.com/work/kaya/)

Creator / platform: Ruckus / studio case study.  
Inspected: case-study hero and brand-guide overview, the dimensional mark on a
black website field, repeated faceted elements, typography pairing, and the
mark's large-scale environmental role.

Visual observations:

- The mark is an architectural object built from crisp planes, bevels, and
  deliberate internal voids, not a glowing blob.
- Thin luminous edges and dark faces create a premium machined/glass character.
- Repeated fragments extend the identity without changing the primary mark.
- High-contrast black presentation lets controlled color and material do the
  expressive work.

Transferable grammar for Codex Director:

- one compact faceted object with a protected central inspection void;
- three planes may imply capability, invocation evidence, and human review
  without becoming three radial spokes;
- deep navy faces, ice-cyan edges, and restrained mint transmission light;
- small controlled bloom only at plane intersections, with most surfaces kept
  crisp and quiet.

Do not copy:

- Kaya's `K`, open-diamond silhouette, nested-square/framed composition,
  blue-orange opposition, or exact bevel geometry;
- loose stacks of identical cards or shards as the primary mark.

What choosing B means: the next proposal will be a precise, sculptural director
object whose refinement comes from faceting, edge light, and a controlled void.  
Trade-off: it feels more premium and product-ownable, but the GPT relationship
will be indirect rather than immediately recognizable.

## Recommendation

Reference Finder recommends **B — Faceted Orchestration Crystal** for the core
identity because it answers the refinement problem while leaving more room for
an ownable Codex Director silhouette. Choose **A** only if immediate familiarity
with current AI-product visual language matters more than collision risk.

Open after selection: the exact silhouette, degree of translucency, and whether
the mark sits on a dark field or a pale native-macOS field.
