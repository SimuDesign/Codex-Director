Superseded on 2026-09-04. Its three monochrome-first directions produced

## Why this revision exists

`CD-LOGO-P01 v1.0` was rejected because the image model was allowed to invent
the mark's silhouette. This pack replaces mood-level prompts with inspected
references and deterministic construction constraints. Third-party references
remain links only; no external artwork is copied into the repository.

## Hard reference gate

No new logo image or production asset may be created until the decision owner
approves one exact lane and pack version.

After approval, the first concept round must be deterministic SVG geometry:

1. black and white only;
2. one 64 x 64 construction grid;
3. no texture, glow, bevel, shadow, or 3D rendering;
4. every anchor, radius, stroke, gap, and symmetry rule documented;
5. proof at 16, 24, 32, 64, and 128 px;
6. the blue-cyan-mint gradient is applied only after silhouette approval;
7. image generation may be used later for presentation mockups, never to define
   the core logo geometry.

## Shared brand and collision constraints

- Preserve the requested blue-cyan-mint rail: `#159DFF` -> `#49CAFF` ->
  `#79EAD8` on dark surfaces. Light-surface solids remain `#0879D9`, `#118EAE`,
  and `#148F7E`.
- Relate to GPT/OpenAI only through high-level qualities: continuity,
  interdependence, balanced negative space, and calm technical precision.
- Do not reproduce the OpenAI Blossom's braided construction, six-part or
  60-degree repetition, central hexagonal counter, or overall silhouette.
- Reject generic AI sparkles, brains, chat bubbles, three-arrow sync rotors,
  steering wheels, shields, power buttons, and open spiral/snail silhouettes.
- The mark must work outside an app-icon container; the macOS rounded square is
  an application surface, not part of the master symbol.

## Lane A — Directed Continuous Path

Thesis: a single controlled path visualises capability discovery, inspection,
and return to action. The GPT relationship comes from continuity and balanced
negative space, not from a knot.

Inspected references:

- [Covariant by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/covariant) — a thin
  looping `C` derived from a decision-boundary flow; useful for linking product
  logic to a restrained continuous gesture.
- [Renaissance by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/renaissance/story)
  — one looping letter gesture carried consistently into the wordmark; useful
  for showing how one signature curve can anchor a family.
- [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) — collision evidence
  only, used to exclude the Blossom's recognizable construction.

Binding construction if selected:

- one non-self-intersecting centerline;
- exactly two terminals, visibly unequal in length;
- exactly three primary turns, all tangent-continuous;
- no rotational symmetry; horizontal bias of 8 degrees;
- central negative space is an off-axis rounded rectangle, never a hexagon;
- minimum internal gap equals one stroke width;
- no arrowheads and no crossing over/under illusion.

Expected strength: closest conceptual relationship to GPT while remaining an
independent silhouette.  
Primary risk: can drift into a generic workflow or letterform mark.

## Lane B — Engineered Director Core

Thesis: the mark is a precise core that reduces complex capability evidence into
one understandable control point. Its distinctiveness comes from a reproducible
formula rather than decorative complexity.

Inspected references:

- [Secondmind by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/secondmind) — a core
  symbol generated from a published 3D mathematical function; useful as the
  precedent for formula-bound rather than prompt-invented geometry.
- [Intrinsic by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/intrinsic) — simple,
  uniform symbol and wordmark paired with robust functional icons; useful for
  separating restrained identity from expressive generative imagery.
- [IBM app icon design](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/app-icons/design/)
  — grid, element-count, fill/stroke, and small-size discipline for app-facing
  symbols.

Binding construction if selected:

- one central circular counter with diameter 16 units on a 64-unit grid;
- exactly three channels, but with angles `205°`, `307°`, and `72°` rather than
  equal 120-degree repetition;
- channel widths follow `8 : 8 : 5`; terminal radii follow `6 : 6 : 4`;
- outer silhouette is open on one side and cannot form a shield, wheel, or
  rounded triangle;
- maximum three visible elements at 32 px; merge to one solid silhouette at
  16 px;
- no radial braid, no spoke hub, and no central hexagon.

Expected strength: most deterministic and closest to the product's "director
core" semantics.  
Primary risk: three channels can still suggest sync or a mechanical rotor if the
asymmetry is weakened.

## Lane C — Split-and-Recombine CD Monogram

Thesis: `C` (capability catalogue) and `D` (director) are constructed as two
interdependent modules. The GPT relationship is reduced to interdependence and
positive/negative-space balance, making the mark more name-ownable.

Inspected references:

- [Abridge by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/abridge) — an `A` split
  into upper and lower structures based on bridge engineering; useful for
  deriving flexible identity from one semantic construction rule.
- [Pienso by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/pienso) — simple geometric
  blocks used as interchangeable product tools; useful for a modular capability
  grammar without a mysterious AI motif.
- [Linear brand](https://linear.app/brand) — a minimal circular mark and compact
  small-size icon treatment; useful as a reduction benchmark, not a shape to
  imitate.

Binding construction if selected:

- exactly two modules and one shared negative-space cut;
- `C` occupies the outer 270-degree arc; `D` is formed by one vertical stem and
  the shared counter, not by a second complete letter;
- module overlap is prohibited; adjacency and negative space create the link;
- one optical axis at 12 degrees; no horizontal/vertical mirror symmetry;
- corner radii limited to two values: 4 and 8 units;
- the silhouette must remain legible as an abstract mark even if the viewer
  does not read `CD`.

Expected strength: most ownable to the Codex Director name and easiest to
reconstruct exactly.  
Primary risk: over-literal monograms can feel corporate or generic unless the
shared counter is distinctive.

## Comparison and recommendation

| Criterion | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GPT-adjacent continuity | Strong | Medium | Medium |
| Codex Director semantics | Medium | Strong | Strong |
| Deterministic geometry | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Name ownability | Low | Medium | Strong |
| Main collision risk | workflow loop | rotor/core | generic monogram |

Brand Designer recommends **Lane C** as the strongest independent identity,
with **Lane A** as the choice if visual proximity to GPT is the dominant goal.

## Inspection coverage

- OpenAI, Linear, Apple, IBM, Google Gemini, and the retained Pentagram case
  studies were reviewed on their source pages.
- Visible reference media was inspected for OpenAI, Linear, Gemini, Secondmind,
  Covariant, Abridge, and Pienso.
- Intrinsic's primary media did not fully load in the inspection browser; it is
  retained only for its explicitly documented separation between simple,
  uniform identity and expressive generative imagery, not for unverified visual
  details.
- Apple and IBM are implementation and reduction references, not aesthetic
  models.
